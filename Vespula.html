Vespula - Yellowjackets, Hornets, etc.

Overview

  • Vespula maculifrons (Eastern yellow jacket) are 12.7-15.9 mm long

Racoons, black bears, and skunks eat them

Integrated Pest Management

Control Methods

Cultural

  1. Strong colony
  2. Entrance reducer
  3. Ensure no cracks or holes in hive bodies
  4. No open feeding
  5. Yellowjacket / wasp traps
