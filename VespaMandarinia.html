Vespa mandarina - Northern Giant Hornet

Overview

The sizes are:

  • Queens - 50 mm long
  • Workers - 35-40 mm long
  • Drones - up to 38 mm long

They have wingspans in the range of 35-76 mm.

There have been few sightings in North America.

Preying mantis will eat them.

Integrated Pest Management

Control Methods

Cultural

  1. Strong colony
  2. Entrance reducer
  3. Ensure no cracks or holes in hive bodies
  4. No open feeding
  5. Yellowjacket / wasp traps
