Achroia grisela - Lesser Wax Moth

Overview

These are 12-13 mm long, with a 12-13 mm wingspan

Good news: Bats eat them.

Integrated Pest Management

Control Methods

Cultural

  1. Strong colony
  2. Entrance reducer
  3. Store equipment properly
  4. Remove unused hive boxes
  5. Add bat houses

Chemical

  1. Store equipment with paradichlorobenzene (PDB) crystals
 

Treatment Methods

Cultural

  1. Freeze any equipment
