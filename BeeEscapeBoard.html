Bee Escape Board

Overview

A bee escape board is used to help reduce the number of bees in a honey super before removing the super from the hive for extracting the honey. They are slow to work though, requiring 24 hours (or so) in place.

Usage Notes

  1. When using it, the triangle goes down.
  2. Plan to leave it on the hive 24 hours.
  3. Do not leave one on for more than 48 hours though as the bees will find their way back thru the maze.
