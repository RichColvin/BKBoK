Viruses

Overview

Viruses are the most difficult diseases to diagnose:

  1. Infected bees are asymptomatic at low levels and only begin showing symptoms at high levels
  2. Symptoms may not be evident in all life stages of a honey bee
  3. Many different viruses can exhibit the same symptoms
  4. Several viruses can be present in a colony at once, making it difficult to interpret symptoms and distinguish the effects of one virus over another. Furthermore, viruses cannot be viewed with a simple microscope like the spores of American foulbrood or Nosema.

Integrated Pest Management

Control Methods

Cultural

  1. Ensure you have strong colonies.
  2. Keep hive tools clean
  3. Limit stressors
  4. Ensure colonies have proper nutrition
  5. Use entrance reducers
  6. Reduce colony drift & robbing
    1. Place hives near landmarks
    2. Spread hives out (10+ yds apart is best)
    3. Orient entrances in different directions

Genetic

  1. Ensure you have a fertile queen. If not a strong colony, re-queen
 

Treatment Methods

Cultural

  1. Discard any infected matting NUCs and cell builders.
