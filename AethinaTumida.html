Aethina tumida - Small Hive Beetle

Overview

These insects are 5-7 mm long, 2.5-3.5 mm wide.

One predator is Pheidole megacephala, commonly known as the big-headed ant in the US.

Integrated Pest Management

Control Methods

Cultural

  1. Strong colony
  2. Emplace hives in full sun so that they stay warm
  3. Remove uneaten pollen patties
  4. Keep apiaries free of combs, cappings, and wet frames
  5. Extract honey from harvested supers.
  6. Beetle trap / jail.
  7. Remove unused equipment
    • Hive boxes
    • Deadouts
 

Treatment Methods

Cultural

  1. Beetle trap / jail.

Chemical

  1. CheckMite+ can be used for treating inside the hive. I have seen little about it and would be quite cautious before using it.
  2. GardStar can be used for treating the ground below the hives (where SHBs pupate). I have seen little about it and would be quite cautious before using it.
