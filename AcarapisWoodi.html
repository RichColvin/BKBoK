Acarapis woodi - Tracheal mites

Overview

These peak in Autumn and Winter.

Integrated Pest Management

Control Methods

Cultural

  • Ensure you have strong colonies
  • Use entrance reducers
  • Reduce colony drift & robbing
    • Place hives near landmarks
    • Spread hives out (10+ yds apart is best)
    • Orient entrances in different directions
  • Ensure hives are not wet inside
  • Keep smaller colonies

Genetic

  1. Requeen using Buckfast queens
 

Treatment Methods

Chemical

  • Mite-A-Thol Menthol is a treatment.

Other

  • Grease patties are an option, though they are messy.
